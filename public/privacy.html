Privacy
PRIVACY

Built to minimize unnecessary data collection.

This starter policy must be reviewed and updated with the legal entity operating the site, its contact details, retention periods, analytics vendors and any CRM or communications providers before production launch.

Valuation information

The calculator can store business category, Texas market, approximate revenue, approximate earnings, growth and business-quality answers in order to calculate and retain the estimate. A visitor who chooses to refine the estimate may also provide the business name, business address and an optional target sale price. Personal contact information is not required to receive the valuation.

Seller conversation requests

If a visitor requests a confidential conversation, the site stores the contact information they submit, their preferred contact method, potential sale timing and the valuation associated with that request. Submitting the form requests contact about a potential business sale; it does not automatically list or market the company to buyers.

Email campaign attribution

Links sent in outbound campaigns may contain a random campaign token. The token can associate the visit with a campaign and limited business prefill information. Email addresses and financial information should not be placed directly in the URL.

Security

Production deployment is designed for Cloudflare Workers with D1, server-side valuation logic, parameterized database queries, security headers, rate limiting and optional Cloudflare Turnstile verification on contact submission. API and CRM credentials are stored as server-side secrets rather than in the browser or Git repository.

Third parties

If licensed benchmark providers, analytics, a CRM, Google Sheets, Slack, email providers or other services are connected, this policy should identify them as required and describe the information shared with each provider. Seller-conversation submissions may be delivered to configured internal lead-notification systems such as a private spreadsheet or Slack channel.
